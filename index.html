About Us

Your wholesale supply partner in Albany, New York

Gans Merchandise Enterprises LLC helps retailers and business customers source everyday merchandise for their shelves. We offer a practical wholesale catalog, straightforward ordering and personal service from our team.

Built for business customers

Our catalog is designed for wholesale buyers. Browse available products, select the boxes you need and send us your business and delivery information through the website.

How Our Website Works

Place your order request in six simple steps

Create an account, choose your wholesale products and send us your business details. Our team will then contact you personally to complete the order.

Create an account

Select “Create Account,” enter your email address and choose a secure password.

Sign in

Sign in to your account so your order request can be saved and shown in your order history.

Browse the catalog

Explore our wholesale catalog, search by product or category and check product availability.

Add at least $200 to your cart

Add the products and box quantities you need. Continue shopping until your cart reaches the $200 USD wholesale minimum.

Enter your details and place the request

At checkout, enter your name, legal business name, phone number, email address and U.S. delivery address. Review your cart and submit your order request.

We contact you within 24 hours

After your request is placed, our team will manually contact you within 24 hours by phone, WhatsApp or email to confirm stock, shipping, payment options and the final order.
